## About

AutoRev Mobilitas Indonesia is building a real vehicle rental operation for individuals and businesses, supported by digital customer and driver experiences, fleet operations technology, and AI-assisted vehicle uptime management.

We are building an integrated ecosystem designed to help individuals and businesses rent, operate, maintain, monitor, and return vehicles to productive use through a simpler and more connected experience.

Our platform vision connects vehicle owners, rental businesses, drivers, internal technicians, workshops, towing providers, parts suppliers, and mobility partners in one operating environment.

AutoRev is currently developing its initial rental operation, technology platform, and founding partner network in Jabodetabek.

Core focus:

- Vehicle rental and electric mobility
- Fleet and rental operations
- Maintenance and vehicle uptime
- Workshop and technician network
- Towing and roadside assistance
- AI-assisted operational intelligence
